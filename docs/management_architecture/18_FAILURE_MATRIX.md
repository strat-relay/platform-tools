# 18 - Failure matrix: observation, evaluation, publication, personal execution

Target design (`08`-`13`). Branches: **P** = product (observation -> decision -> publication), **X** = personal execution (translator/executor), **E** = evidence. "Fail closed" means: no decision/effect is produced on unsound input rather than a guess.

| # | Failure | Fail closed? | Retry? | Quarantine? | Other branches continue? | Evidence impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Observation producer unavailable** (TOS down) | yes: no observation, no decision | restart / new instance resumes from `observation_seq` in PostgreSQL | no | P idle for all trades; X and unrelated services unaffected; **alert on "no observation for > stale limit"** (this is the silent failure of today's path) | gap interval recorded per trade (`OBSERVATION_GAP`); managed track applies no decision during the gap; FORWARD series flags the trade as `INCOMPLETE_OBSERVATION` |
| 2 | **MarketDataProvider unavailable** | yes: producer emits nothing; never substitutes another provider/feed silently | provider adapter retries with backoff; health `DEGRADED` | no | as 1 | as 1 |
| 3 | **Stale market data** (quote age > provider bound, or observation older than the version's `max_market_age_ms`) | yes: producer refuses to publish a stale quote; evaluator turns a stale delivery into `HOLD(STALE_MARKET_DATA)` | new observation on next poll | no | yes | persisted `HOLD` with reason (auditable); no managed-track change |
| 4 | **Duplicate observation** | n/a (idempotent) | - | no | yes | none (inbox hit / `observation_id` unique) |
| 5 | **Out-of-order observation** | yes for the affected trade: later `seq` waits for its predecessor | redelivery with backoff up to `max_deliver`; predecessor read from `trade_observation` | after bound: trade parked `OBSERVATION_GAP` | other trades continue | gap recorded; no decision from the out-of-order item |
| 6 | **JetStream unavailable** | producer commits to PostgreSQL + outbox (state stays true); consumers wait | relay publishes when back; **no file fallback** | no | P decisions stall (nothing to consume); the product record stays consistent; X unaffected | staleness policy converts long delays into `HOLD(STALE)`/gap; nothing back-dated |
| 7 | **PostgreSQL unavailable** | yes: producer cannot allocate `observation_seq`; evaluator cannot commit | consumers do not ack; JetStream redelivers | no | X fails closed too (A5); other domains per their modes | gap; no decision, no ack |
| 8 | **Evaluator crash before DB commit** | n/a | JetStream redelivers; recompute yields the same `decision_id` | no | yes | none |
| 9 | **Crash after DB commit, before ACK** | n/a | redelivery -> inbox hit -> ACK | no | yes | none (decision persisted once) |
| 10 | **Malformed observation** (schema/`payload_hash`/`bars_ref.digest` mismatch, negative spread, ask < bid) | yes: no decision | bounded redelivery | **yes**: `quarantine` row + DLQ subject; trade flagged `OBSERVATION_QUARANTINED` | yes (poison acked after quarantine) | gap + reason; alert |
| 11 | **TM policy exception** in the pure evaluator | yes: no decision from this observation | retried up to `max_deliver` (deterministic - retry rarely helps) | **yes** after bound: trade flagged `EVALUATION_FAULT`; version flagged; alert | other trades continue (fault isolated **per trade**, unlike today's `process_once` where one exception aborts the loop) | gap; a persisted `EVALUATION_FAULT` marker; the managed track stalls, never guesses |
| 12 | **ManagedTrade missing** for an observation | yes | limited (creation may lag its own event) | after bound: quarantine `MANAGED_TRADE_MISSING` | yes | none for an unknown trade; alert if it recurs |
| 13 | **TM version missing / manifest mismatch** (bound version not found, code manifest differs from running files) | **yes - hard**: refuse to evaluate for that version | no (configuration problem) | trade parked `TM_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE`; evaluator health `UNAVAILABLE` for that version | other versions/trades continue | gap; decisions never produced by an unverifiable evaluator |
| 14 | **Personal execution unavailable** (translator down / account off / bridge off) | X only | translator resumes from its durable consumer | no | **P continues unchanged** (decisions, publication decisions, management signals) | none for product evidence; LIVE evidence not produced |
| 15 | **Customer distribution unavailable** | Distribution only | Distribution retries from `signal.management.published.v1` | no | **P and X continue** (Trading Core has no dependency on Commerce) | none; delivery status is Commerce's evidence |
| 16 | **Broker state stale** | X only: `execution_eligibility = NOT_ELIGIBLE(BROKER_STATE_STALE)` | on next snapshot | no | P continues | none for product; the decision stands and is not suppressed |
| 17 | **Observation source switches during an open trade** (provider or feed change) | yes: the trade's reference feed is fixed at open (`05`); a different `feed_id` is **not** applied to a bound trade | operator action: explicit `feed_rebind` decision recorded (not automatic) | observations from the unexpected feed recorded as `FEED_MISMATCH` and not evaluated | other trades on the original feed continue | series flagged `FEED_CHANGE`; a trade that changes feed mid-life is excluded from clean managed series unless explicitly re-labelled |
| 18 | **Producer restart / duplicate producers** | seq allocation serialised by row lock/lease; second producer's inserts conflict | safe | no | yes | none |
| 19 | **Publication gate error** (Signals unavailable) | publication only: no `ManagementSignal` | gate retries from `trade.decision.made.v1` | no | decisions and X continue | published series delayed; decision timestamps unaffected |
| 20 | **Executor primitive unavailable** (bridge refuses REAL close/trail; adapter lacks `MOVE_TO_BREAKEVEN`/`PARTIAL_CLOSE`) | X: `NOT_EXECUTABLE(<primitive>)` recorded on the effect | re-evaluated when the primitive exists | no | **P continues; decision never suppressed** (`13`) | none for product; personal LIVE management unavailable |

## Reading the matrix

* **Fault isolation per trade**: an exception or poison message affects one ManagedTrade, never the loop (contrast `stream_consumer.process_once`, where one exception ends the pass and a `MISSING_QUOTE` silently skips everything).
* **Silence is an alert**: rows 1-2 and 7 exist because the current system *cannot tell* "no data" from "nothing to do".
* **Branch independence**: rows 14-16 and 19-20 are the operational form of "entitlement affects distribution only, personal execution may be off".
