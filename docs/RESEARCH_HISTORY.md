## Superseded geometry result

Earlier Phase 4/5 metrics used cases where opposing structure was behind the
entry and an absolute target distance could mask the sign. Those metrics are
`SUPERSEDED_GEOMETRY_BUG`. The corrected Phase 5C results are
`EXPOSED_DEVELOPMENT_ONLY`; they are not validation evidence.

The correction preserves signed reward, rejects targets behind/at entry,
keeps extension-consumed cases visible, and distinguishes re-entry before
target completion from return after the originating target completed.

## Phase 7 — passive management observer

Phase 7 preserves exact frozen V1 entries and observes alternative path and
position-management hypotheses in a separate process. It has its own freeze,
state, JSONL ledger, heartbeat, PID, and manifest. It does not change V1 or
turn shadow hypotheses into a selected exit policy. Its prospective boundary
is the timestamp in `context_structure_retrace_phase7_manifest.json`.

## Signal orchestration shadow platform

The orchestration layer is infrastructure, not a new strategy. Its first
enabled adapter is Context V1. It preserves strategy signal performance,
account sizing, and distribution as separate evidence streams. The initial
orchestrator freeze and runtime artifacts are under `runtime/orchestration/`.
